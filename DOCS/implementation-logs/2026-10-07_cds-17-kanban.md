## Technical Decisions

| Decision | Rationale | Alternatives rejected |
|---|---|---|
| Header-only cards (owner) | One place for item detail, the backlog's Items sections | `DOCS/kanban-content.json` with descriptions |
| Type and Tier filled, Priority `—` (owner) | Tier gives the portfolio status report its HIGH, MEDIUM and LOW counts | All three; minimal |
| Exact devDependency plus a `verify` step (owner) | Locked, works offline, and covered by CI and the registry gate with no other change | `npx` in `verify` (needs the network); a CI step only |
| Commit only, no Pages (owner) | Publishing waits for the evidence update after CDS-18 | Pages now |
| `Open` for unfinished items | Ready and Backlog are derived by the generator; writing them in the backlog would be a second, contradictable truth | Keep `READY TO START` and `BLOCKED` in the cells |

## Lessons Learned

- **A graph-derived board needs explicit edges.** The order brief 7 set (CDS-18 after CDS-17) existed only in prose; on the board it had to become a "Blocked by" entry.
- **Check the exit code of the command, not of the pipe.** `cmd | tail; echo $?` reports `tail`.
- **Read the clock before writing a stamp, again.** The backlog v31 header said 09:05Z and `verify.mjs` said 09:10Z, but both were committed at 09:02Z. Both are corrected in the records PR.
